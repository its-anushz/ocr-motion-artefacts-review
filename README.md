# Impact of Motion Artefacts on OCR Accuracy

Review paper analyzing the effect of motion blur and image degradation on Optical Character Recognition (OCR) performance in camera-captured document images.

## Overview

This work studies how motion blur, out-of-focus blur, and capture distortions affect OCR accuracy in mobile-captured documents. The paper reviews OCR enhancement techniques, blur estimation approaches, BLSTM-based OCR systems, and benchmark datasets used for document quality assessment.

Published in:
**International Journal of Progressive Research in Engineering Management and Science (IJPREMS)**  
Volume 04, Issue 01, January 2024

## Authors

- Anushree K C
- Akshatha M

## Key Topics Covered

- OCR challenges in camera-captured documents
- Motion blur and out-of-focus blur analysis
- BLSTM-based OCR systems
- OCR accuracy prediction using local blur estimation
- SmartDoc-QA dataset analysis
- Image preprocessing and enhancement techniques
- Quality assessment for mobile document capture
